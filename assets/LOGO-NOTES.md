# Modern Dark logo

`logo-modern-dark.png` is the proposed master mark for the Modern Dark UI.

- Three horizontal bars represent the ordered mod stack.
- The broken outer ring suggests sonar and scanning.
- The lower wave establishes the naval theme without using a literal ship.
- The cyan, sea-green, and blue palette is intended for charcoal UI surfaces.

The existing application icon remains unchanged until the new mark is approved.

## Alternate concepts

- `logo-modern-dark-minimal-stack.png` — simplified ring and stack for maximum small-size clarity.
- `logo-modern-dark-sonar-pulse.png` — radar sweep and pulse arcs emphasize scanning.
- `logo-modern-dark-tidal-layers.png` — staggered bars flow into the lower wave.
